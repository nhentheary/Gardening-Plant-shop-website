Quick Care Tips

The four things every plant needs to stay happy.

Watering

Most plants prefer to dry out slightly between waterings. Overwatering is the #1 killer.

Sunlight

Match the plant to the light in your space. Most indoor plants prefer bright indirect light.

Humidity

Tropical plants love moisture. Group plants together or use a pebble tray to boost humidity.

Pest Control

Check leaves weekly. Neem oil or insecticidal soap handles most common houseplant pests.
